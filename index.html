Booking FAQs

Frequently answered queries regarding regional transits between Chandigarh and Dehradun.

Are state tolls and highway charges included in the Chandigarh to Dehradun fare?

Yes, all estimated prices include state border taxes (Chandigarh UT, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, and Uttarakhand), highway toll fees, driver allowances, and fuel overheads.

Can you drop me directly at Jolly Grant Airport or Mussoorie Diversion?

Absolutely! Whether your destination is ISBT Dehradun, Rajpur Road, IT Park, Jolly Grant Airport (DED), or the Mussoorie Diversion road, our drivers provide direct doorstep drop-offs without any city routing hassles.

Can I request a stop at Paonta Sahib Gurudwara along the route?

Yes! If you wish to take a brief break at Paonta Sahib for Darshan or refreshments during your drive from Chandigarh, simply inform your driver during booking for minimal or zero waiting charges.
